# What Is Percentage?

A percentage is a way of expressing a number as parts of 100.

For example:

- 50% means 50 out of 100.
- 25% means 25 out of 100.
- 100% means the whole amount.

Percentages are widely used in mathematics, business, finance, shopping, education, and statistics.

## Percentage Formula

Percentage = (Part ÷ Whole) × 100

## Example

If 20 students out of 40 passed:

Percentage = (20 ÷ 40) × 100 = 50%
